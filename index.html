🎹 Vibe Coding Keyboard — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2, 2026

Summary: Vibe Coding Keyboard does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, keystrokes, or text you type. Everything stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

None. Vibe Coding Keyboard operates entirely on your device. We do not collect, log, or transmit:

  • Keystrokes or text input
  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Device identifiers or usage analytics
  • Location data
  • Contacts or address book data
  • Clipboard contents
  • Browsing history or app usage

2. Keyboard Permissions

Android requires Input Method Editor (IME) apps to request special permissions to function as a keyboard. When you enable Vibe Coding Keyboard, Android displays a standard warning that the keyboard "may be able to collect all the text you type, including personal data like passwords and credit card numbers."

This is a standard Android system warning shown for ALL third-party keyboards. Vibe Coding Keyboard does NOT collect any text you type. All processing happens locally on your device.

3. Data Storage

Your keyboard preferences and settings (such as theme selection, snippet configurations, and layout preferences) are stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the app

4. Voice Input (Optional Feature)

If you use the optional voice input feature, audio is processed through your configured transcription endpoint (e.g., your own n8n webhook). We do not operate or have access to your transcription server. You are responsible for the privacy practices of your own transcription endpoint.

5. Third-Party Services

Vibe Coding Keyboard does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection SDKs. No data is shared with any third party.

6. Network Access

The keyboard does not require or use internet access for its core functionality. Network permission, if present, is only used for the optional voice transcription feature when explicitly activated by the user.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
